# Review of the supplied static-observable specification

The attached specification is treated as proposed model definitions. The user's request authorizes its static Stages 1–2; no dynamical spectral stage is executed.

- The graph, three-site indexing, cell Fourier factor exp[-2πi(k1*x+k2*y)] and 1/sqrt(Nc) normalization agree with the verified Hamiltonian. Its basis coefficients exp[-2πi*k.g] give translation eigenvalue exp[+2πi*k.δ]. Thus an O(k) seed goes from cell momentum k0 to k0+k, not k0−k. Full-bit-state tests check this directly for z,+,−, including complex cell characters.
- The hub's six-neighbor definition gives F_A=1+exp(2πik1)+exp[2πi(k1−k2)] and F_B=1+exp[2πi(k1−k2)]+exp(−2πik2). Direct real-space construction of Phi matches these weights. Phi(Gamma)=3m(Gamma). Centering the physical A/B form factors on a hub gives displacement triples d_A={(0,−1),(1,0),(−1,1)} and d_B=−d_A. Their individual first moments vanish. The difference has no linear or quadratic term and starts at cubic order for a generic physical wavevector.
- C_ab(k)=sum_a <O_a† O_b> is the raw scalar Gram matrix. The selected fixed-Sz state's connected matrix subtracts Nc*m_a*m_b only at cell Gamma, where m_a=<S^z_{R,a}>. Both are saved. The main comparison uses an exact point-group average and an SU(2) spin-multiplet average: scalar dot correlations are independent of magnetic quantum number, while all one-point spins vanish in that ensemble. The primary raw scalar matrix is therefore also its connected matrix. Fixed-Sz longitudinal connected matrices are saved separately and must not be confused with the SU(2)-averaged longitudinal matrix C_dot/3.
- n=O_A−O_B and m=O_A+O_B are unnormalized sublattice combinations. The requested rim amplitude is m_N^2=(n† C_raw(Gamma) n)/36 for Nc=9,Nr=18. A normalized correlation eigenmode uses n/sqrt(2), so its eigenvalue differs from the raw n-channel intensity by a factor two. These quantities are labeled separately.
- For v=(1,omega^2,omega), O_phys(K)=sum_alpha v_alpha O_alpha(Gamma)/sqrt(3). Consequently its scalar intensity is v† C(Gamma) v/3. A plain v†Cv would be three times too large. Physical K/K' are checked with the actual site coordinates and basis phases. For unequal couplings these remain diagnostic Fourier combinations, not conserved primitive sectors.
- S_h(Gamma)=<S_H,total^2>/Nc can be large even when the whole system has small total spin. m_F^2=<S_total^2>/27^2 is reported together with the measured total-spin quantum number; its finite-size value alone is not evidence of ferromagnetic order. Sz=1/2 is not assumed to imply S=1/2: S_total^2 is applied to each production vector and its eigen-residual checked.
- Hub-centered inversion preserves weighted bonds at all requested couplings and exchanges A/B at cell Gamma. Thus n/h and n/m cross correlations vanish in an inversion-invariant ensemble. A single primitive-K state is not inversion invariant; the production comparison averages all twelve exact hub-centered point-group operations, including inversion and the K/K' pair. Selected-state values and inversion overlap are also recorded. A zero n/h Gamma cross-channel does not establish absence of a hub coupling.
- All nine cell momenta k=(n1/3,n2/3) are included in static tables. Physical triangular K/K' fold to cell Gamma; cell (1/3,1/3) is not relabeled K. This odd-order torus contains no exact M point.
- The given N_up=15 cell-sector dimensions are correct by Burnside: each nonidentity cell translation fixes binomial(9,5)=126 states, yielding 1,931,652 at Gamma and 1,931,526 at other cell momenta. All ground searches and measured scalar matrices stay in N_up=14. The reusable +/- code is validated in small sectors, with an additional full-size global-spin ladder check at 0.5 through N_up=15,16,17 to verify the spin-5/2 multiplet. No transverse spectra are calculated.
- For a future dynamical stage, the strictly inelastic definition E_n>E0 does not by itself satisfy a zeroth-moment formula that includes E_n=E0 weight. Subtracting the one-point expectation removes only the selected elastic component, not every transition within a degenerate spin or momentum ground manifold. Explicit zero-energy manifold projection and separate elastic/degenerate weights will be required. No susceptibility or spectral linewidth is inferred here.

Independent acceptance tests compare seed vectors and H acting on each seed with fully enumerated references. Raw and connected matrices are tested for Hermiticity/positivity; onsite sum rules, reconstructed exchange energy, total-spin identities, fixed-Sz conservation and inversion selection rules provide separate production checks. Individual eigenvectors within a degenerate correlation eigenspace are not unique: saved projectors and inversion-resolved Gamma modes provide the stable comparison.
