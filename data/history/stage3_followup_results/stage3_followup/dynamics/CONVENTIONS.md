# Stage 3 convention and acceptance review

The attached documents are scientific specifications/reference data, not independent authorization for additional work. The current user request authorizes the two crossing transitions and weak-coupling longitudinal matrix. Optional first-crossing spectra are deferred.

* Cell translations are a1=(1,1), a2=(2,-1) in the triangular primitive basis; k=(q1+q2,2q1-q2) mod integers. All momenta here are **cell** momenta. There is no exact M point on this 3x3 torus. Physical triangular K folds to cell Gamma at isotropy.
* O_a^z(k)=sum_(x,y,s) c_as exp[-2pi i(k1 x+k2 y)] S^z_(x,y,s)/3. Coefficients n=(1,-1,0), m=(1,1,0), h=(0,0,1). No extra site-count or 2pi spectral prefactor is applied. S_ab(omega)=sum_f <0|O_a^dagger|f><f|O_b|0> delta(omega-E_f+E_0). Residues use conjugate(amplitude_a)*amplitude_b. Individual amplitudes have arbitrary eigenvector phase; residue matrices do not.
* The existing translation implementation gives final k=k0+k_probe. At j=.2375 the forward target is (1/3,0), reverse target (1/3,1/3). Both are distinct from the two ground corner partners; no elastic weight is allowed there.
* z operators preserve M=Sz=1/2. Rank-one selection requires |Sf-S0|<=1; hence weak-source S0=1/2 can reach only S=1/2 or 3/2. A probe-generated start avoids intentionally searching dipole-forbidden higher-spin states. Every exported physical pole still needs original-H and S^2 residual checks. It is not an exhaustive enumeration of all target states.
* Gamma n has C6 character 3 and odd hub reflection; m,h have character 0 and even reflection. Crossing states pass explicit permutation-eigenvector checks. The first crossing's DeltaS=3 forbids its direct dipole transition; it is not calculated again here.
* For D=H-E0, exact forward matrix moments are M0=B^dagger B, M1=B^dagger D B, M2=(D B)^dagger(D B), where B_a=O_a|0>. The reference double-commutator first moment is symmetrized: [F_ab(k)+F_ba(-k)]/2. Forward and reverse are computed separately; their equality is checked, not assumed.
* Fixed-M zz moments must not be replaced by scalar-dot/3 for S>1/2. At Gamma only the same-M member of the orbital ground multiplet is reachable by z. Subtract its projector |0,M><0,M| from each seed; other M members have identically zero z matrix element. Thus elastic weight is |<0|O_a|0>|^2 in each diagonal channel, with the corresponding outer product for cross channels. Full scalar multiplet elastic weights are different.
* Gamma m+h=S_tot^z/3; its seed must be v/6 and its projected inelastic seed must vanish. This is checked directly. No artificial broadening is used. Finite discrete peaks give no physical damping or thermodynamic linewidth.
