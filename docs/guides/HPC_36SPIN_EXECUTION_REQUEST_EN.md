## Hamiltonian and geometry: preserve exactly
H = J sum_(rim AB nn) S_i·S_j + J' sum_(hub-rim nn) S_i·T_R; J=1, r=J'/J, spin-1/2. Unit cell includes A, B, H (3 sites); each H has 6 rim neighbors; each rim has 3 H and 3 rim neighbors. NO hub-hub edges. The cell basis is ((1,1),(2,-1)) in the physical triangular primitive coordinates. Site IDs 3*cell_index+s, s=0(A),1(B),2(H). The recommended 36-spin 12-cell torus has T1=(2,2), T2=(-2,4) in CELL coordinates; determinant 12. Verify the torus metric, bond counts (36 rim bonds + 72 hub-rim = 108 total), no duplicate bonds, translation automorphisms and all cell momentum sectors. The weak-coupling cell k0=(1/3,2/3) and physical triangular K=(1/3,2/3) are NOT the same momentum coordinates; physical K folds to cell Gamma. At isotropy verify the independent 36-site triangular bond construction.

## Important scientific interpretation
18 and 27 spins show the same qualitative hub polarization -> rim compensation mechanism, but level-crossing positions vary strongly by size and shape. The exact N=27 crossing near r=0.2577 is NOT a demonstrated bulk critical point. Uniform hub polarization is not automatically spontaneous ferromagnetic LRO. Goldstone damping is not inferred from discrete finite-cluster ED levels.
